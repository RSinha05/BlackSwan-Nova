Mathematical Framework
Geometric Brownian Motion · Box-Muller transform · Cholesky decomposition
GBM Stochastic Differential Equation
dS = μ·S·dt + σ·S·dW

Discrete solution:
S(t+Δt) = S(t)·exp[(μ − σ²/2)Δt + σ·ε·√Δt]

ε ~ N(0,1), dt = 1/252 (daily)
Parametric VAR Formula
VAR(α,1d) = Investment × (z_α·σ_d − μ_d)

z_α = Φ⁻¹(α) [1.645 @ 95%, 2.326 @ 99%]
σ_d = σ/√252, μ_d = μ/252

VAR(T days) = VAR(1d) × √T
CVaR / Expected Shortfall
CVaR = E[Loss | Loss > VAR]

Parametric:
CVaR = φ(z_α)/(1−α) × σ_d × Investment

Simulation: mean of worst (1−α) outcomes
Box-Muller Transform
U₁, U₂ ~ Uniform(0,1)

Z₁ = √(−2·ln U₁)·cos(2π·U₂)
Z₂ = √(−2·ln U₁)·sin(2π·U₂)

Both Z₁, Z₂ ~ N(0,1) — exact generation
